## Questions people actually ask

**Is Uniswap the same thing as UNI?**
No. Uniswap is the decentralized trading protocol; UNI is its governance token. Buying UNI doesn't give you equity in anything — it gives you a governance asset and a liquid token.

**Can I buy UNI without completing verification?**
Gate's guidance ties higher limits and broader payment options to completed KYC, and requirements differ by region and method. Assume you'll verify; don't plan around skipping it.

**Which payment method is cheapest?**
For anything above a few hundred dollars, bank transfer plus a spot order. Card purchases are the most expensive route on the board at roughly 1–5%, and they exist for speed and convenience, not for value.

**Do I need to pay with GT to get a discount?**
Only if you're below VIP 10. Between VIP 0 and VIP 9, GT payment shaves about 0.01 percentage points off each side. From VIP 10 up, the columns are identical and the discount disappears.

**What's the minimum purchase?**
Minimum order sizes vary by trading pair and by payment provider. The order form shows the applicable minimum for your selected route — if a small order bounces, that's almost always the reason.

**Should I buy UNI as a limit order or market order?**
Market fills instantly at the taker rate. A limit order pays the maker rate, which is lower from VIP 4 onward, but may not fill. If you want in today and the amount is small, the fee difference is fractions of a cent either way.
